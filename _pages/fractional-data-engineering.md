# Fractional Data Engineering Services

**Experienced data engineering. Fixed sprint fees. Flexible commitments.**

Your data engineering backlog keeps growing, but hiring another full-time engineer isn't always practical.

Empty Cup Consulting provides experienced data engineering capacity through fixed-fee, three-week delivery sprints. We help organizations build pipelines, improve data quality, modernize data platforms, and address technical debt without adding permanent headcount.

[Schedule an introduction call](https://calendly.com/jjhconsulting/presales-call) to discuss your engineering backlog and capacity needs.

## Plan for 12 Weeks. Commit to Three.

We organize delivery into **12-week product increments**, each consisting of **four three-week sprints**. A product increment gives your team a shared objective and a prioritized roadmap. You purchase **one sprint at a time**, with a fixed fee and defined engineering capacity.

At the end of each sprint, we review completed work, discuss what changed, and adjust priorities. You decide whether to renew for the next sprint. The 12-week roadmap guides planning; future sprints and their deliverables depend on renewal and available capacity.

## What We Can Help With

- **Data pipelines and integration:** Ingestion, ETL/ELT, orchestration, and change data capture.
- **Data modeling and warehousing:** Dimensional models, transformations, and query performance.
- **Data quality and reliability:** Validation, testing, monitoring, and troubleshooting.
- **DataOps:** Source control, CI/CD, deployment automation, and engineering standards.
- **Platform modernization:** Practical engineering support for Microsoft Fabric, Azure, Snowflake, Databricks, and related technologies.
- **Technical debt:** Refactoring, operational improvements, and maintainability.

## How Each Sprint Works

1. **Plan:** Agree on a sprint goal and prioritize work that fits the reserved capacity.
2. **Build:** Engineer, test, review, and document the work, with visibility into progress and blockers.
3. **Review:** Demonstrate completed work, discuss outstanding items, and refine the backlog for a possible next sprint.

## Choose the Capacity That Fits

We offer multiple fixed-capacity tiers, from targeted part-time support to a more embedded engineering resource. The sprint fee and capacity are agreed before work begins. Architecture guidance, coordination, and engineering delivery are planned within that capacity.

**Fixed-fee capacity provides budget predictability; it does not guarantee completion of an unlimited or fixed-scope backlog.**

## What You Get

- Predictable three-week sprint fees and a clear capacity commitment.
- Experienced engineering with practical architecture guidance.
- A prioritized backlog and transparent delivery process.
- Reviewed code, testing, documentation, and regular demonstrations, as appropriate to the work.
- Flexibility to continue, adjust capacity, or stop at a sprint boundary.
- A rolling 12-week roadmap that connects engineering work to business priorities.
