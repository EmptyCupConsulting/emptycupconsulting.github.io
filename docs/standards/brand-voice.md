# EmptyCup Consulting — Brand Voice

## Our Philosophy

The EmptyCup name draws inspiration from Eastern philosophy, particularly the value of approaching problems with an open mind.

Curiosity, humility, and a willingness to challenge assumptions are central to how we work. Experience gives us perspective, but it doesn't mean we have all the answers.

Eastern philosophical quotes, stories, and ideas are part of our brand identity. Use them occasionally when they offer a relevant insight or reinforce a point. They should complement the content, never compete with it.

Don't force philosophical references into technical explanations, marketing copy, or other content where they feel out of place. Verify quotes and their attribution.

## Our Voice

Write like an experienced technical practitioner talking to a capable colleague.

Be direct, credible, conversational, and willing to express informed opinions. Question conventional wisdom, explain tradeoffs, and offer practical advice grounded in experience.

Use dry humor, occasional sarcasm, and candid observations when appropriate. Challenge unnecessary complexity and industry hype without belittling clients or people learning something new.

Be confident without being arrogant. Stay open to being wrong.

## Our Audience

We write primarily for SMBs, IT leaders, data professionals, and technical decision-makers.

Respect their intelligence and time. Focus on useful insights, concrete examples, and recommendations they can act on.

## Our Marketing Approach

Demonstrate expertise by helping readers understand and solve real problems.

Connect technical decisions to business value. Be specific about how EmptyCup's assessments, consulting, training, and workshops can help.

Include clear, relevant calls to action in marketing content without turning everything into a sales pitch.
